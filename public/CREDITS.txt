Generated samples: original text-only imagegen compositions for draped cloth, parkour, inclined wall, linen folds, statue, terracotta and an ivory sports coupe. The public release omits earlier close screenshot recreations and the source-photo-based car. Every built-in photo has its own imagegen relative-depth interpretation. Normals and combined maps are derived from that depth with Mapshroom utilities. Depth Anything V2 Small remains available for user photos and regeneration. Hashes and dimensions are provided in each sample's provenance.json. See ASSET_PROVENANCE.md for prompts and origin details. No claim is made that AI geometry is calibrated or that purely generated elements have exclusive copyright.

Photographs are processed in the browser. The model is downloaded from Hugging Face; fonts load from Google Fonts. Hosting providers receive ordinary page and asset requests. The app does not upload user photos to an inference service.
