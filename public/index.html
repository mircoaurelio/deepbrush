INDEPENDENT EDUCATIONAL DEMO

Paint along the surface.

Created by Mirco Aurelio. This experimental implementation is inspired by the publicly demonstrated behavior of Adobe’s Project Deep Brush. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supplied by Adobe.

“Reverse-engineered” here means behavioral reimplementation from a public demonstration. No Adobe source code was obtained, copied or decompiled in this project. This demo estimates relative depth and normals; it does not reproduce Adobe’s proprietary implementation or full feature set.

Choose Flat, Tilt or Warp. Drag to paint; use B for brush, E for eraser, T for text and L for light. Import a matching combined map or generate one on your device. Use Undo to remove a stroke.

Your photos are processed locally in your browser. The depth model downloads from Hugging Face on first use, and fonts load from Google Fonts. There is no server-side photo upload.
