# Sample provenance

The samples in this release use original text-only image generation or the separately credited CC0 photographs. No Adobe screenshots or screenshot-based photo recreations are included. Each of the nine gallery photographs now has its own imagegen relative-depth interpretation; normal and combined maps are derived from that same map with the included Mapshroom geometry utilities. The generated depth is approximate and can differ from fine photo edges; matched dimensions do not establish calibrated accuracy. Depth Anything V2 Small remains available for in-browser regeneration and user uploads. Generated imagery and geometry are approximate; no exclusive rights in purely generated elements are claimed.
